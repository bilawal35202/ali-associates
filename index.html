About Us

Ali Associates & Traders is a professional construction, architecture, and project management company established in 1998. With decades of experience, we specialize in residential, commercial, industrial, and government sector projects.


Our team of skilled engineers, architects, and project managers is committed to delivering innovative designs, quality construction, and exceptional project execution. From concept to completion, we transform ideas into reality with precision and professionalism.


We believe in creating modern living spaces with durable structures, premium finishing, and customer satisfaction at the core of every project.


Our services

Grey Structure Construction Services

We specialize in delivering high-quality grey structure construction with precision, durability, and engineering excellence. Our grey structure services cover the complete structural framework of your building, ensuring a solid foundation for all future finishing work.

Architecture & 3D Design

Custom architectural designs, master plans, 3D elevations, and working drawings tailored to your vision and requirements.

Interior Design Services

We create functional, elegant, and personalized interior spaces that reflect your lifestyle and vision. From concept to execution, our team transforms ordinary spaces into modern, comfortable, and aesthetically pleasing environments.

Renovation & Finishing

Complete renovation, up-gradation, and finishing services for homes, offices, hospitals, and commercial properties.

Project Management & Supervision

End-to-end project supervision and construction management ensuring timely delivery, quality control, and budget compliance.

Steel Fabrication

Expert fabrication of steel canopies, oil tanks, multi-purpose sheds, and structural steel works with precision craftsmanship.
